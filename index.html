SoDUCKu

One duck per row, column, and patch. No ducks touching.

How to play

Rules

  • Every row gets exactly one duck.
  • Every column gets exactly one duck.
  • Every colored patch (outlined in thick lines) gets exactly one duck.
  • No two ducks touch, not even diagonally.

Mouse and touch

  • Tap a square once for an X (no duck here), twice for a duck, three times to clear it.
  • Drag from an empty square to mark several squares with X.

Keyboard

  • Tab to the board, then use the arrow keys to move.
  • Space or Enter: cycle empty, X, duck.
  • D: place or remove a duck. X: place or remove an X. Delete: clear.
  • H: hint. U or Ctrl+Z: undo.
  • R: read the whole board row by row.

Levels load from levels.json, the same format as CS 149 PA4.
